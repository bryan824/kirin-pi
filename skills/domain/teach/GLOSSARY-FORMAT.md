# GLOSSARY.md Format

`GLOSSARY.md` is the canonical language for the teaching workspace. All
lessons, exercises, and learning records should adhere to its terminology.

## Structure

```md
# {Topic} Glossary

{One or two sentences describing the topic this glossary covers.}

## Terms

**Progressive overload**:
Systematically increasing the demand on a skill or body over time.
_Avoid_: Pushing harder, levelling up

**RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)**:
A 1–10 self-rating of how hard a set felt, where 10 is failure and 8 means two
reps left in the tank.
_Avoid_: Effort score, intensity rating
```

## Rules

- **Add a term only when the user understands it.** The glossary records
  compressed knowledge; it is not the place where the user first learns it.
- **Be opinionated.** Pick the best term and list sloppy aliases to avoid.
- **Keep definitions tight.** One or two sentences that define what the term is.
- **Use glossary terms inside other definitions** once they exist.
- **Group under subheadings** when natural clusters emerge.
- **Flag ambiguities explicitly.** When a term is used loosely in the wider
  field, record the resolution: "in this workspace, 'set' always means a working
  set — warm-ups are tracked separately."
- **Revise in place** as understanding deepens; do not leave stale definitions.
